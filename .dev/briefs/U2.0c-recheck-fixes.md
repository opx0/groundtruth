# U2.0c — what the recheck found in the fixes

Two independent rechecks ran over the four fixed template files: one rendered
the whole matrix again, one re-audited every printed field against the agency's
own column description. Between them they found five defects that the fixes
themselves introduced or left, and two that the first audit missed. The kernel
and the record kinds have already been changed where the fixes need it.

Both rechecks agreed on the priority order. Work through it.

## Already done for you

1. **`Requirement` gained `atLeast`.** `{ slot, present: true }` is satisfied by
   the value `0`, which is how a template named for noncompliance came to
   render over five facilities that have none. `SectionFilter` has had
   `atLeast` since it was written; the new mechanism was given three of the
   four comparisons it needed. Use `{ slot, atLeast: 1 }`.
2. **`fema-flood-zone` gained `sfhaFlag: Sourced<string | null>`** — `SFHA_TF`
   read a third time, verbatim, with the `identity` transform, beside the
   boolean and the mapped label.
3. **The `frsActiveStatus` comment in `lib/evidence/records.ts` was wrong and
   is corrected.** See finding 6.

## The findings

### `lib/templates/sems.ts`

**1. `sems-site/npl@1`'s requirement is wrong in both directions.**
`{ slot: "semsNplStatus", present: true }` is satisfied by any value of
`npl_status_name`, and the recorded bytes hold three: `Currently on the Final
NPL` (2 sites), `Site is Part of NPL Site` (1), `Not on the NPL` (12). The
committed test records this as passing output:

> `VALERO PLUME is listed by SEMS as Not on the NPL, 0.76 km from the mapped point.`

rendered from the template whose whole purpose is B7's separate sentence for
sites on the final National Priorities List. Thirteen of fifteen sites are
eligible for a list the count excludes them from.

Require the value: `{ slot: "semsNplStatus", equals: "Currently on the Final NPL" }`.
That is the same string the selection policy's NPL section filter will use, so
the count and the list cannot describe different sets. `equals` is safe here —
`semsNplStatus` is plain null off a failed join, and `satisfies` fails `equals`
on a null slot; read it and confirm that for yourself.

Note in the module comment what this cannot see: a site on the final NPL whose
Envirofacts request failed has a null Superfund status and gets no NPL
sentence, while `frsActiveStatus` survives that failure carrying
`CURRENTLY ON THE FINAL NPL`. That is honest — it is not known from the
inventory — and the section trace lists the ids counted, so the two can be
reconciled. Do not add a second template for it in this unit.

**2. Delete `sems-site/disagreement@1`.**

It declares `{ state: "statusRow", is: "joined" }` while its stated premise is
that the two agencies disagree, and on the recorded data they never do. See
finding 6: `ACTIVE_STATUS` on this layer is the Superfund interest's own
status, equal to `npl_status_name` upper-cased on 15 of 15 sites. A template
selected by a case-folding comparison the kernel cannot see is exactly the
hazard `Requirement` was added to remove, and an exact `differsFrom` would fire
on capitalisation alone.

This file's own header already says what happens without it: the summary prints
the Superfund inventory's answer and the trace carries every other field
including the registry's. Delete the template, delete it from `semsTemplates`,
and write the reasoning into the header — including that it comes back, with a
requirement that can express it, the day a recorded record actually disagrees.

**3. `Status:` renames the column, and "as of Date unavailable." is not
English.** Current:

```ts
sentence`Status: ${field("nonNplStatus")}, as of ${fallback(field("statusDate"), "Date unavailable")}.`
```

renders, on PRSI FIRE:

> `PRSI FIRE, 5.14 km. Not on the NPL. Status: Removal Only Site (No Site Assessment Work Needed), as of Date unavailable.`

Two problems. The column is `non_npl_status_name` — the **non-NPL** status —
and labelling it `Status:` while the NPL status above it carries no label at
all makes the reader take the labelled one as the site's status. And B10's
"Date unavailable." is a standalone sentence; inlined after "as of" it produces
a mid-sentence capital and a phrase with no meaning. Split into two clauses so
a null date takes its own clause and nothing else, and label both statuses.

**4. Four of five lead clauses print a distance with no origin.**
`lib/templates/echo.ts` states the rule and applies it to all of its lead
clauses, citing this file: `sems-site/npl@1` has said "from the mapped point"
since it was written and the other four have never said it. Apply it.

**6. `ACTIVE_STATUS` belongs to an interest, not to the site.** The layer's own
field description, in the `fields` array of
`tests/fixtures/sems/arcgis-5mi-houston.json`:

> `ACTIVE_STATUS`: "The status of **the environmental interest** at the facility
> or site (e.g., Active, Inactive, Operating, Proposed for NPL, Deleted from
> Final NPL, Currently on Final NPL)."
>
> `INTEREST_TYPE`: "The environmental permit or regulatory program that applies
> to the facility site."

So `EPA's facility registry lists it as SITE IS PART OF NPL SITE` attributes an
interest-level status to the site. The record carries the interest —
`interestType`, `SUPERFUND (NON-NPL)` or `SUPERFUND NPL` — and no template
prints it. Print the interest the status belongs to:

```ts
sentence`EPA's facility registry records its ${field("interestType")} interest as ${field("frsActiveStatus")}.`
```

Read the whole `fields` array yourself before rewriting; two findings came
straight out of it and there may be more.

Also fix what `registry-only@1` now says. Its `no-row` state means the
inventory answered with nothing, and the clause before it hands the reader the
inventory's own NPL vocabulary under the registry's name. With the interest
named, that reads correctly; check that it does.

### `lib/templates/echo.ts`

**5. `noncompliance@1`'s requirements are both wrong.** It declares
`[{ slot: "complianceStatus", present: true }, { slot: "quartersInNoncompliance", present: true }]`.
`present` is satisfied by `0`, and five of the seven recorded rows have
`FacQtrsWithNC` of `"0"`, so the template renders:

> `CARGILL INCORPORATED, 0.22 km from the mapped point. Quarters of noncompliance in ECHO's twelve-quarter history: 0. …`

Its own docstring calls it B7's second ECHO group and `sections.ts` counts that
group as "{count} of them have **at least one** quarter". Use
`[{ slot: "quartersInNoncompliance", atLeast: 1 }]` and drop the
`complianceStatus` requirement entirely: it makes the quarters count hostage to
a different column, so a row with six quarters and a null status would be
refused by `noncompliance@1` and `summary@1` alike and fall to `no-status@1`,
which says nothing about quarters. Let the status clause drop on its own, as
every other null does.

**7. The penalty-amount hedge dropped the one word the column carries.**
Current: `Penalty amount column in ECHO's facility summary: $0.` The column is
`FAC_LAST_PENALTY_AMT` — the **last** penalty's amount. The rewrite removed
"Most recent" to stop the old wording asserting a zero-dollar penalty and in
doing so dropped the scope the column name states, so it now reads as the
facility's penalty amount in general. The same fixture's own query header says
`"TotalPenalties": "$1,250"`, so a reader taking `$0` as the total is
contradicted by the response it came from. Name the scope:
`Amount of the most recent penalty in ECHO's facility summary: $0.`

**Not a finding, and do not change it.** The recheck argued that "twelve-quarter
history" is unsupported because `tests/fixtures/echo/metadata.json` returns
`"Description": null` for every column. That is a reason not to claim a window
for `FAC_PENALTY_COUNT`, which is why "on record" was removed. It is not a
reason to drop the twelve-quarter window for `FAC_QTRS_WITH_NC`, whose name is
literally "quarters with noncompliance" out of ECHO's published definition of
the column as the last twelve quarters — the same class of published-EPA
evidence this repo accepted for the AQS response envelope and the FEMA column
descriptions. Keep the wording, and add a line to the module comment saying
where the window comes from: ECHO's published column definition, not the
metadata endpoint, which describes nothing.

### `lib/templates/fema.ts`

**8. The parcel caveat was moved off the one card that needed it.** It now
appears nowhere on the flood card, and a test asserts its absence. But the zone
clause is the only sentence in the product that asserts a hazard designation
**at a point**; A5 requires each card to carry its limits on the card, and C2
forbids "Parcel-level flood risk". The reason it was moved — that inside a
clause whose only slot was `zoneCode` it explained nothing when clicked — was a
reason to put it where it qualifies a slot, not to remove it. Now that the
clause carries `sfhaLabel` too, it does qualify one:

```ts
sentence`The mapped point, a street-segment interpolation rather than a parcel boundary, is in zone ${field("zoneCode")}, ${fallback(field("sfhaLabel"), "…")}.`
```

Keep the record caveat as well, and keep the test that asserts it.

**9. The fallback does not meet B10's unknown-status rule.** B10: an unknown
source status is shown **verbatim**, with "Meaning not mapped." The current
fallback says the mapping failed and points at the trace, because no slot
carried the raw letter. One now does: `sfhaFlag`. Split into two templates, each
declaring the state it speaks about — which also gives this file the
requirements its header currently argues it does not need:

```ts
// requires [{ slot: "sfhaLabel", present: true }]
sentence`The mapped point … is in zone ${field("zoneCode")}, ${field("sfhaLabel")}.`
// requires [{ slot: "sfhaLabel", present: false }]
sentence`The mapped point … is in zone ${field("zoneCode")}. FEMA's Special Flood Hazard Area flag recorded for this area is ${fallback(field("sfhaFlag"), "empty")}. Meaning not mapped.`
```

Two templates each declaring their state is not the hazard your header feared.
The hazard was two templates asserting opposite phrases with no requirement at
all. Rewrite that paragraph of the header to say so.

## Scope

Three agents, three disjoint sets. Yours is named in your task: one template
file and its test file, nothing else.

Do not edit `lib/evidence/**`, `lib/adapters/**`, `docs/**`, `.dev/**`,
`tests/fixtures/**`, another agent's files, or any test outside your own. The
kernel changes and the new `sfhaFlag` field are already in place.

Note that `tests/unit/evidence/scopes.test.ts` and
`tests/unit/evidence/render.test.ts` exercise `lib/templates/sems.ts` and you
may not edit them. If deleting `disagreement@1` breaks them, say so precisely
in your report — which test, which line, what it asserted — and stop; do not
work around it.
